Vocal Commands

VAYU's real-time WebRTC audio pipeline allows you to naturally speak to the AI and trigger web-based data fetching and messaging.

■ web_search

Searches the web for any information. Connects to multiple search engines (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yandex) to provide real-time facts during conversation.

■ flight_finder

Searches Google Flights and speaks the best options. Retrieves real-time pricing, duration, and stop-over information directly via voice command.

■ youtube_video

Advanced YouTube integration. Can perform multiple actions: play, summarize, get_info, or fetch trending videos directly on your desktop.

■ weather_report

Gives the weather report to user by connecting to the OpenWeather API. Instantly fetches current conditions and forecasts for any location.

■ send_message

Sends a text message via WhatsApp, Telegram, or other messaging platform autonomously using voice dictation.

■ Simultaneous Duplex

Interrupt the AI while it's speaking. Because VAYU uses WebRTC VAD, it instantly stops transmitting audio when it detects you speaking over it.
